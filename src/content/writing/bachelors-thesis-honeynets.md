My final Computer Science project looked at how to defend energy-management
infrastructure — the systems behind smart grids — using honeynets: decoy
environments built to attract, detect, and study attackers.

The work was part of a larger research effort, so there's only so much I can
share publicly. In short: I designed and deployed deception infrastructure that
captured real attack behaviour against smart-grid systems and turned it into
useful defensive signal. It was graded 10 / 10 with Distinction.
